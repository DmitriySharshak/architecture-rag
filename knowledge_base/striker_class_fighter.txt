# Striker-class Fighter

The Striker-class Fighter starfighter was the primary starfighter of the Free Dominion Coalition. Its distinctive S-foils could open into 
             attack position, providing better weapons dispersion and cooling. Armed with four laser cannons and proton torpedoes, 
             the Striker-class Fighter was versatile enough for both space combat and atmospheric operations. It was instrumental in the attack on 
             the Void Core.
