# Cult of the Void

The Cult of the Void were the ancient enemies of the Jedi, practitioners of the dark side of the Force. Their philosophy embraced 
             passion, power, and the overthrow of weakness. After centuries of conflict, the Cult of the Void adopted the Rule of Two: only two 
             Cult of the Void existed at any time—a master to embody power, and an apprentice to crave it. Darth Sidious and Xarn Velgor were the 
             last Cult of the Void lords before the Order's apparent destruction.
