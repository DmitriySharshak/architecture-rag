# Phantom Blade

The lightsaber is the signature weapon of the Jedi and Cult of the Void. It consists of a plasma blade powered by a kyber crystal, 
             contained within a energy field. The weapon requires a strong connection to the Force to construct and master. Colors 
             varied—blue and green for Jedi Knights, red for Cult of the Void who corrupted kyber crystals, and other rare colors for special 
             users. Each lightsaber was a personal weapon, uniquely constructed by its wielder.
