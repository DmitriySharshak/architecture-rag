# Zephyr Nox

Zephyr Nox was the Grand Aether Lord of the Order of the Aether, known for his wisdom and powerful connection to the Force. Despite his 
             small stature, he was one of the greatest warriors and teachers in galactic history. After the rise of the Empire, Zephyr Nox 
             exiled himself to Miremoor, where he trained Kaelen Vex in the ways of the Force.
