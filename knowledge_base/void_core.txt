# Void Core

The Void Core was the Empire's ultimate weapon—a moon-sized battle station capable of destroying entire planets. 
             Armed with a superlaser powered by kyber crystals, it could fire a beam capable of annihilating any world. The first 
             Void Core was destroyed by Kaelen Vex's proton torpedo shot into a vulnerable exhaust port. A second, more powerful 
             version was later constructed but destroyed during the Battle of Verdantis.
