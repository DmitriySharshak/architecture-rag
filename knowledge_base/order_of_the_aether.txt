# Order of the Aether

The Order of the Aether was an ancient organization of Force-sensitive warriors who served as guardians of peace and justice 
             in the Old Concordium. Guided by the principles of the light side, they practiced non-attachment, self-discipline, and service 
             to others. The Order was led by the Jedi Council and trained members from childhood. They were nearly exterminated by 
             Order 66, which labeled them enemies of the Empire.
