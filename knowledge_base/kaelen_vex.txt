# Kaelen Vex

Kaelen Vex was a farm boy who became a legendary hero. He discovered his connection to the Force through 
             training with Aether Lord Eldrin Thorne and later with Aether Lord Zephyr Nox. Luke wielded a blue lightsaber and later constructed 
             his own green blade. He played a crucial role in destroying the Void Core and redeeming his father, Damon Vex, 
             from the dark side.
