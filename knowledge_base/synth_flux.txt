# Synth Flux

Synth Flux is an energy field created by all living things that binds the galaxy together. It can be sensed and 
             manipulated by those with special training. Synth Flux has two aspects—the light side, associated with selflessness and 
             harmony, and the dark side, associated with emotion and aggression. Jedi sought to serve the light side, while Cult of the Void 
             exploited the dark side for power.
